P8

A radio in your Mac's menu bar. Eight stations, one keystroke.

Download for Mac macOS 14 or later · free · signed & notarized

About

Eight hand-picked independent and public stations from Copenhagen, Seattle, Los Angeles, London, Paris and Madrid — one click and one keystroke away. P8 shows the song that's playing when the station publishes it, sends audio to AirPlay speakers including Sonos, and follows your keyboard's play/pause key.

No account, no ads, no tracking. P8 plays each broadcaster's own public stream; audio and track information belong to the broadcasters.

Install

Download P8-<version>.zip, double-click it, and move P8 to your Applications folder. The first time you open it, macOS asks whether you want to open an app downloaded from the internet — say Open.

Help

Where is it?
Click the radio icon next to the clock. If you don't see it, your menu bar may be full — open System Settings › Menu Bar and make sure P8 is allowed.
Keys
With the panel open, 1–8 switch stations and space pauses. Your keyboard's play/pause key works too, and P8 shows up in Control Center's Now Playing.
AirPlay and Sonos
Click the AirPlay control in the panel's footer and pick a speaker. Sonos speakers appear there as AirPlay 2 speakers.
A station says "unavailable"
The broadcaster's stream didn't answer after several retries. Click it to try again; P8 also retries by itself when your network comes back.
No song title for some stations
Some broadcasters don't publish what's playing. P8 shows the station name instead.
Updates
P8 checks once a day and offers new versions; Settings › Updates checks on demand. To update, download the new zip, quit P8, and replace the copy in Applications.
Launch at login
Settings (the gear icon) › Launch at login.
